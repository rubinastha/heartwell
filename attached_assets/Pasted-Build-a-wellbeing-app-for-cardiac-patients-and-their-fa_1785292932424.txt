Build a wellbeing app for cardiac patients and their families. Make it fully 
functional — real data should save and persist, not just look interactive.

CORE CONCEPT
Users search or browse cardiac terms/diagnoses, save the ones relevant to 
them as "saved words" with an optional personal note, and log daily 
check-ins (mood, symptoms, medication adherence) that can be tagged to a 
saved word. All of this rolls up into a timeline and a summary view.

SCREENS

1. Word lookup
- Search bar to find a cardiac term or diagnosis
- Result card: term name, a plain-language explanation (2-3 short 
sentences), a "why this matters" note
- "Save to my saved words" button — when saving, prompt the user for an 
optional personal note (e.g. "this is what my cardiologist told me in 
March") and store it alongside the saved word

2. Daily check-in
- Mood selector (3-5 simple face icons)
- Checklist of common symptoms (chest tightness, fatigue, shortness of 
breath, dizziness)
- Toggle for "took medication today"
- Option to tag the entry to one of the user's saved words
- Optional short note field
- Save button

3. Timeline
- A single feed combining saved words and journal entries in chronological 
order, newest first
- Filter chips: All / Saved words / Entries
- Each row is clickable — clicking any entry opens a detail view showing 
the full content: for a saved word, the explanation and any personal note; 
for a check-in, the full mood/symptoms/medication/note data and the date 
it was logged

4. Summary
- A date range picker (e.g. last 7 days, last 30 days, or custom range)
- Once a range is selected, show aggregate stats calculated from check-ins 
in that range: mood trend over time, how often each symptom was logged, 
medication adherence rate (% of days marked "took medication"), and total 
number of check-ins
- Keep the visualizations simple — bar or line charts are fine

5. Profile
- A section where the user can enter and edit: their name, current 
medications (list, with the ability to add/remove), condition or diagnosis, 
and their doctor's name and contact info (phone/email)
- This is a single-user profile, not a login/account system — no 
authentication needed, just a form that saves this information

STYLE
Calm and reassuring, not clinical or alarming. Soft neutral background, one 
accent color (teal or soft blue), generous white space, rounded corners, 
minimal clutter. No red or warning colors for normal states. Mobile-first, 
single-column layouts throughout, with simple bottom or side navigation 
between the five screens listed above.

SCOPE
No login or account system. No notifications. No care-team messaging. Just 
these five screens, working navigation between them, and real data that 
persists.
